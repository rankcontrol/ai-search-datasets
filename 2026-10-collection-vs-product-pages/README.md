# Collection pages vs product pages (October 2026)

We checked what Google ranks for 8 everyday product searches in October 2026, such as "stand mixer," "hiking backpack 50l," "dog food for sensitive stomach" and "noise cancelling headphones." We took the top 10 organic results for each, 66 in all, and sorted them by page type. Separately, on October 19, 2026 we ran 8 buying questions through OpenAI's API with web search on, one run each, and tagged the pages ChatGPT cited.

- **Write-up:** [Collection Pages Beat Product Pages 23 to 1: Ecommerce SEO in 2026](https://medium.com/@thesearchshift/collection-pages-beat-product-pages-23-to-1-ecommerce-seo-in-2026-cfcf14127c0c)

## Files

| File | What it holds |

## Results

| Page type in Google's top 10 | Results | Share of 66 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Independent reviews and guides | 23 | 35% |
| Store collection pages | 23 | 35% |
| Forums and video | 11 | 17% |
| Store and brand guides | 8 | 12% |
| Single product pages | 1 | 2% |

Stores held 32 of the 66 spots, nearly all of them collection pages or guides. The one product page that ranked was a backpack page named exactly what the shopper had typed. The collection pages that ranked were named after the search too, like "50L backpacks" and "sensitive stomach dog food," and not after an internal category.

Google also showed product listing units on 7 of the 8 searches and an AI Overview on 7 of 8. All 7 AI Overviews cited Reddit.

ChatGPT cited 34 pages. 24 of them, 71%, belonged to brands and stores, and 15 of those were product pages. It cited exactly one collection page. Review publishers such as RTINGS, Food Network and Runner's World got 5 citations, and health and safety bodies such as the AAD and OSHA got 5. Of the 43 searches ChatGPT ran, 19 went straight to a single brand's site. ChatGPT looked at Reddit plenty, 73 of the 390 sources it read, but none of the 34 pages it cited was a Reddit thread.

## Limits

8 searches on Google and 8 questions for ChatGPT, run once each. ChatGPT's questions were similar to the Google searches but not the same wording, so the two tests don't line up one to one. The ChatGPT run used OpenAI's API rather than the ChatGPT app. Only the tallies are here, not the row-level list.
