# AI citations by page format (September 2026)

We put 24 buying questions to OpenAI's API, with its web search tool on, and to Perplexity's API on September 26, 2026. Twelve were software questions, about help desks and CRMs, and twelve were about services such as accountants and business insurance. The next day we opened every page the answers cited, 308 in all, and tagged each by format.

- **Write-up:** [We Sorted 308 AI Citations by Format. Here's What Gets Cited.](https://medium.com/@thesearchshift/we-sorted-308-ai-citations-by-format-heres-what-gets-cited-f9251c238f68)

## Results

| Page format | Software | Services | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Roundup or alternatives page | 118 | 21 | 139 |
| Product or service page | 14 | 45 | 59 |
| Explainer or how-to | 4 | 53 | 57 |
| Head-to-head comparison | 10 | 12 | 22 |
| Review site or marketplace listing | 11 | 0 | 11 |
| Official docs or government page | 1 | 8 | 9 |
| Pricing page | 7 | 0 | 7 |
| Single-product review or other | 2 | 2 | 4 |
| **Total** | **167** | **141** | **308** |

Software and services came out almost opposite. Roundups and alternatives pages were 118 of the 167 software citations. On services questions they were 21 of 141, and explainers (53) plus the firms' own service pages (45) carried most of it. 85 of the 118 software roundups came from companies selling in that category.

Per engine, about half of Perplexity's 230 citations were roundups. OpenAI's 78 citations were 28% roundups and included 12 comparison pages, one of them Intercom's page comparing itself with Help Scout.

Help Scout's pricing page loads with annual billing selected and doesn't print "annual" beside $25, $45 or $75. Google's AI Overview and Perplexity repeated those prices without the word.

## How we sorted

A page that lists several options went in as a roundup even when its title said guide. Two or three named products side by side went in as a comparison. A page selling one company's own thing went in as a product or service page, whatever the title.

## Limits

We ran each question once or twice, through the APIs rather than the ChatGPT app, so this is a snapshot. Only the tallies are here, not the row-level list.
