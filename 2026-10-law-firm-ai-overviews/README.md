# Law firm searches: map vs AI Overview (October 2026)

In October 2026 we checked what Google showed for 8 legal searches: 2 hiring searches, such as "personal injury lawyer Houston" and "divorce lawyer Denver," and 6 question searches, such as "how long does a divorce take in Colorado" and "can I get a DUI expunged." For each we noted whether Google put an AI Overview (the AI-written answer box above the results) on the page. For the 6 overviews we sorted the 18 sources they named, called citations, by site type. We also asked ChatGPT once what a personal injury lawyer costs.

- **Write-up:** [SEO for Law Firms: 11 of 18 AI Overview Citations Went to Firm Sites](https://medium.com/@thesearchshift/seo-for-law-firms-11-of-18-ai-overview-citations-went-to-firm-sites-401e2a86fb3a)

## Results

| Site type cited in the 6 AI Overviews | Citations |
|---|---:|
| Law firm websites | 11 |
| Government sites | 3 |
| Other sites | 3 |
| Legal directory | 1 |

All 6 question searches got an AI Overview and neither hiring search did; on the hiring searches the page opened with a map of 3 firms, followed by firm sites. Of the 7 citations that were not law firms, the 3 government sites were Colorado courts, the Texas insurance department and the Florida revenue department, the 3 other sites were Reddit, a custody app and an ignition interlock company, and the one directory was Super Lawyers.

We found the 11 firm pages ordinary: each answered one question, and none came from a big national name. A Florida firm's child support explainer was cited beside the Florida revenue department's page, and a Colorado firm's piece on divorce timelines shared an overview with the state courts.

Asked once what a personal injury lawyer costs, ChatGPT searched for typical contingency fees and for the bar association, then cited 2 American Bar Association pages and no law firm. In the keyword data, "personal injury lawyer near me" had about 301,000 US searches a month at about $163 a click, while "divorce lawyer near me" had 40,500 at about $25.

## Limits

8 Google searches and 1 ChatGPT question, all in October 2026, so 18 citations is a small count and the hiring result rests on 2 searches. Search volume comes from Google search volume estimates and click price from Google Ads data. Only the tallies are here, not the row-level list.
